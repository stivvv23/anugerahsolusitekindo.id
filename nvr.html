HD NVR Premium

The Avigilon HD NVR Premium is a high-performance, high-capacity recorder that offers high storage density. The NVR Premium line provides up to 1670 Mbps of total throughput for demanding enterprise video management environments. It is pre-configured with Avigilon Control Center (ACC)™ high-definition video management software, recording video data in a highly-available RAID 6 hard drive configuration.

HD NVR Standard

The Avigilon HD NVR Standard provides high performance and high available storage. The NVR Standard line provides up to 900 Mbps of total throughput. It is pre-configured with ACC high-definition video management software, recording video data in a highly-available RAID 6 hard drive configuration.

HD NVR Value

The Avigilon HD NVR Value delivers high performance and retention, while accommodating deployments looking for a flexible solution at a lower cost of ownership. The NVR Value line provides up to 384 Mbps of total throughput. It is pre-configured with ACC high-definition video management software, recording video data in a highly-available RAID 5 hard drive configuration.

HD NVR2 storage expansion

Avigilon’s HD NVR2 storage expansion can be used to expand retention duration while ensuring the safekeeping of all of your HD footage. Both the hard drives and power supplies are hot-swappable for online repairs. The storage expansion has up to a 30TB effective recording capacity, all in a 2U rack mount chassis.
